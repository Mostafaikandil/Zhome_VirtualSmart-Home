What can I control?
Lighting
Choose Warm glow, Reading, or Bright. Fine-tune the brightness and white tone.
Cooling
Turn cooling on or off and change the target temperature. The simulated room temperature responds over time.
Music
Choose an ambient track, adjust volume, or pause. Audio starts after you tap.
Curtains
Choose Close, Half, or Open, or drag the slider to any position.
Presence
Use Simulate vacancy to trigger Away. Simulate arrival brings back Welcome Home.
